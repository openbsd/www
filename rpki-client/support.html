rpki-client Professional Support

Rpki-client is mission-critical open-source software which protects the safety and reliability of the global Internet routing system.

Organisations wishing to establish a direct contractual relationship for professional support for rpki-client can do so through the Route Server Support Foundation.

Revenue is used to fund rpki-client's ongoing development.
